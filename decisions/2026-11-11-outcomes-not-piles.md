# Organize work by outcome projects

Decided: 2026-11-11

Rule: When something new starts, ask whether it is a project (one outcome with a finish line), a parent
      issue or a single issue, with a recommendation. Every worked ticket sits in the outcome project it
      serves, or its start comment says why none fits. Projects express commitments, labels express
      categories, views express questions.

Reason: The tracker had grown into piles of issues grouped by topic. The operator could not see from the
        record which outcomes were committed, how far each was, or what waited on them. A layer of
        outcome projects with acceptance criteria makes the commitments visible and lets a steward keep
        them current.

Reopen when: The views over the record answer "what is committed, how far is it, who acts next" without
             the project layer.

Effect: New work is shaped before it is filed; the Linear Steward keeps outcome projects and each
        ticket's current-state block true.
