# Use the issue tracker as the live decision spine

Decided: 2026-08-11

Rule: Keep execution work and formal deliberation in the issue tracker. Put small rulings on
      their work issue and preserve settled reasons in the decision archive.

Reason: The estate needed a live ledger that agents could query, update, and hand off through;
        passive traces or labels alone could not carry a decision lifecycle.

Reopen when: The tracker fails a concrete lifecycle or handoff need, and a replacement meets
             it without creating competing live queues.

Effect: Work ownership and open decisions share a discoverable live surface.
