# Pair live deliberation with a sealed archive

Decided: 2026-08-13

Rule: Use the issue tracker for a decision while it is active and a searchable local corpus
      for its final reason. Keep the corpus available to new agents.

Reason: A path-addressed file alone could not carry the live lifecycle, while tracker-only
        history was harder for a new agent to inspect locally at any time.

Reopen when: One surface proves it can provide both the live lifecycle and reliable local
             discovery without duplicate authority.

Effect: Decision files preserve the why; the tracker retains the current state.
